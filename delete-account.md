# Delete your RevX account

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

This page is for the **RevX** app, the official app of the RevX motorsport event at RevX KFUPM (developer: RevX KFUPM, Dhahran, Saudi Arabia). It explains how to delete your RevX account and what happens to your data.

**Contact:** revxtechsupport@gmail.com

---

## 1. Delete your account in the app

1. Open **RevX** and sign in.
2. Tap **Profile** in the bottom bar.
3. Tap the **Settings** (gear) button at the top right.
4. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
5. Tap **Delete account** again to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and permanently, and you are signed out. The account's data is also removed from your phone. This can't be undone.

If you only used RevX as a guest, there is no account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your phone.

## 2. Request deletion without the app

If you can't open the app or no longer have it installed:

1. Email **revxtechsupport@gmail.com** from the email address you used for your RevX account.
2. Use the subject **Delete my RevX account**, and include that email address in the message.
3. We may reply to ask you to confirm the request, so we don't delete someone else's account.

We delete the account and all the data listed below within 7 days of confirming your request, and never later than 30 days after you contact us. We'll email you when it's done.

## 3. What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete:

- **Account details:** your email address, your password (stored only in encrypted form) and your sign-in sessions.
- **Profile:** your name, username, KFUPM major or department, favorite driver, and appearance and notification settings.
- **Profile picture:** the photo file itself, from our storage.
- **Points, badges and rankings:** your points, badges and medal, and your place on the leaderboards and in Community.
- **Journey:** the stops you completed and the stop codes you entered, including incorrect attempts.
- **Queues:** your Car Show and Karting queue tickets and their history.
- **Games:** your game results and best scores.
- **Votes and reminders:** your competition votes and the session reminders you turned on.
- **Notifications:** your notification tokens and the personal notifications we prepared for you, such as reminders and queue calls.

## 4. What is kept, and for how long

| Data | Why it's kept | How long |
|---|---|---|
| A record that a staff action took place, for example "a queue turn was checked in" | Event security and fair play | Kept, but it no longer contains anything that identifies you. Your account ID is removed from it when your account is deleted. |
| Technical request logs kept by our hosting provider (Supabase), which can include your IP address and account ID | Security and troubleshooting | Deleted automatically by Supabase within 7 days |
| Notifications already delivered to your phone | They're on your device, not with us | Until you clear them or uninstall the app |

We don't keep backup copies of the RevX database, so deleted accounts are not kept in backups. If we turn on automatic backups in the future, backup copies will be deleted within 7 days.

We keep nothing else about your account after it is deleted.

## 5. Deleting some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove some data:

- **Profile picture, name and other profile details:** edit or remove them under **Profile → Edit profile**.
- **Notifications:** turn them off in the app's **Settings** or in your phone's settings.
- **Session reminders:** turn a reminder off by tapping **Reminder on** in the session's details.
- **Anything else:** email **revxtechsupport@gmail.com** and tell us what you want deleted. We reply within 30 days.

For more about the data RevX uses, see our [Privacy Policy](https://shahadomarr.github.io/RevX-KFUPM/privacy-policy).
