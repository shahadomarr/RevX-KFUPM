## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us
- **Account details:** your email address, a password (stored only in encrypted form by our sign-in provider), your name, and a username.
- **Optional profile details:** your KFUPM major or department, a profile picture you choose from your photos, and a favorite driver.
- **Your settings:** privacy and visibility choices, appearance, and notification preferences.

### Information created when you use the app
- **Journey progress:** which event stops you complete, when, and how (stop code or staff check-in), and stop codes you enter, including incorrect attempts.
- **Queues:** when you join, leave or are called in the Car Show and Karting queues, and whether you confirmed your turn or were marked attended.
- **Points, badges and games:** points you earn, badges you unlock, and your game results (such as scores and reaction times).
- **Votes and reminders:** votes you cast in event competitions, and session reminders you turn on.

### Information from your device
- **Notification token:** if you allow notifications, your phone receives a token so we can send you messages (for example, "It's your turn" in a queue). We also store your device platform and app version.
- **Photos:** the app only accesses a photo when you choose one as your profile picture. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

### What we don't collect
- We don't track your location.
- We don't use your camera or microphone.
- We don't use advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell your information.

## 2. How we use your information
- To create and run your account.
- To run the event features: the Journey, queues, stop codes, points, badges, games, leaderboards, voting and the agenda.
- To send notifications you have allowed, such as queue updates, session reminders and event announcements.
- To keep the app fair and secure, for example by limiting repeated wrong stop codes.
- To let event staff manage queues and check in attendees.

## 3. What other people can see
- **Other attendees** can see your name, username, profile picture, points, rank and badges on leaderboards and in Community, depending on your privacy settings. You can change what is shown under **Profile → Settings**.
- **Event staff** can see your name and username when you are in a queue they manage, so they can call you and check you in.
- Your email address is never shown to other users.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **Delete your account:** in the app, go to **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your account, profile, points, journey progress and related data.
- **Edit your profile** at any time under **Profile → Edit profile**.
- **Turn notifications off** in the app's settings or in your phone's settings.
- **Control your visibility** under **Profile → Settings**.
- **Contact us** at [CONTACT EMAIL] to access, correct or delete your information, or with any privacy question. We will reply within [30] days.

Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, you may have additional rights, including the right to access, correct and request deletion of your personal data.

## 7. Children
The app is intended for event attendees aged [13] and older. We don't knowingly collect information from younger children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is restricted so that each user can only read and change their own information, and staff can only use the tools needed for their role. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.
